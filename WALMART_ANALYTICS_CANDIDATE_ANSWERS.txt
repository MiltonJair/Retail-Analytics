PREGUNTA 7: SQL QUERY - RETRIEVE INFO FOR QUESTIONS D & E (ALL STORES)
================================================================================

-- Top 10 Selling Stores Overall & Top 20 Best-Selling Items (All Stores)

SELECT 
    'Top 10 Stores' as Category,
    s.StoreName,
    s.State,
    NULL as ItemName,
    SUM(id.LineTotal) as TotalSales,
    SUM(id.QuantitySold) as TotalQuantity,
    RANK() OVER (ORDER BY SUM(id.LineTotal) DESC) as Rank
FROM InvoiceHeaders ih
INNER JOIN InvoiceDetails id ON ih.InvoiceID = id.InvoiceID
INNER JOIN Stores s ON ih.StoreID = s.StoreID
GROUP BY s.StoreID, s.StoreName, s.State
HAVING RANK() OVER (ORDER BY SUM(id.LineTotal) DESC) <= 10

UNION ALL

SELECT 
    'Top 20 Items' as Category,
    NULL as StoreName,
    NULL as State,
    i.ItemName,
    SUM(id.LineTotal) as TotalSales,
    SUM(id.QuantitySold) as TotalQuantity,
    RANK() OVER (ORDER BY SUM(id.LineTotal) DESC) as Rank
FROM InvoiceDetails id
INNER JOIN Items i ON id.ItemID = i.ItemID
GROUP BY i.ItemID, i.ItemName
HAVING RANK() OVER (ORDER BY SUM(id.LineTotal) DESC) <= 20

ORDER BY Rank ASC;

-- Alternative Query: Top 20 Items Per Store (Shows item performance in each store)

SELECT 
    s.StoreName,
    d.DepartmentName,
    i.ItemName,
    SUM(id.LineTotal) as TotalSales,
    SUM(id.QuantitySold) as TotalQuantity,
    RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY s.StoreID ORDER BY SUM(id.LineTotal) DESC) as StoreRank
FROM InvoiceHeaders ih
INNER JOIN InvoiceDetails id ON ih.InvoiceID = id.InvoiceID
INNER JOIN Stores s ON ih.StoreID = s.StoreID
INNER JOIN Items i ON id.ItemID = i.ItemID
INNER JOIN Departments d ON i.DepartmentID = d.DepartmentID
GROUP BY s.StoreID, s.StoreName, d.DepartmentID, d.DepartmentName, i.ItemID, i.ItemName
HAVING RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY s.StoreID ORDER BY SUM(id.LineTotal) DESC) <= 20
ORDER BY s.StoreName, StoreRank ASC;

PREGUNTA 8: FORECASTING METHOD FOR SALES PREDICTION
================================================================================

RECOMMENDED METHOD: Hierarchical Time Series Forecasting

RATIONALE:
Sales forecasting at Walmart requires handling multiple hierarchical levels:
   Level 1 (Highest): Total Sales (All Stores)
   Level 2: Sales by State (CA, TX, NY)
   Level 3: Sales by Store (1000 stores)
   Level 4: Sales by Department (7 departments per store)
   Level 5 (Lowest): Sales by Item (26 items per department)

HIERARCHICAL FORECASTING APPROACH:

1. BOTTOM-UP METHOD (Most Accurate for Item-Level):
   - Generate independent forecasts for each Item at each Store
   - Aggregate forecasts upward through hierarchy
   - Formula: Total Forecast = SUM(Store[i] Forecasts) = SUM(Dept[j] Forecasts)
   - Advantage: No reconciliation needed, preserves lower-level patterns
   - Best for: Inventory management, SKU-level planning

2. TOP-DOWN METHOD (Aggregate First):
   - Forecast total sales for all stores
   - Disaggregate using historical proportions
   - Formula: Item[i] Forecast = Total Forecast × (Historical Item[i] % of Total)
   - Advantage: Captures macro trends, good for budget allocation
   - Best for: Revenue forecasting, executive reporting

3. RECONCILIATION METHOD (Optimal):
   - Generate independent forecasts at ALL levels
   - Apply reconciliation algorithm (MinTrace, ERM) to ensure coherence
   - Result: All forecasts are coherent AND maintain hierarchical relationships
   - Advantage: Best statistical properties, optimal for mixed-level decisions

SPECIFIC ALGORITHMS:

Primary: Prophet (Facebook)
   - Robust to missing data and outliers
   - Handles seasonality (daily, weekly, yearly for retail)
   - Interpretable components (trend, seasonality, holidays)
   - Fast training: <1 second per series
   - Implementation: 3 months of data sufficient for good forecasts

Secondary: ARIMA(p,d,q)
   - Classical statistical approach
   - Excellent for short-term forecasts (1-7 days)
   - Requires stationary data (differencing)
   - Works well with 90+ days of history

Tertiary: Exponential Smoothing (ETS)
   - Simple, fast, good for stable patterns
   - Suitable for 30-day rolling forecasts
   - Less flexible with trend/seasonality changes

IMPLEMENTATION STACK:
   - Python: Prophet + statsmodels
   - Frequency: Daily forecasts for next 30 days
   - Update: Daily retraining with new data
   - Accuracy: MAPE target <10% for same-store comparisons

EXPECTED RESULTS (Based on 90-day historical data):
   - Total Sales Forecast (30 days): $712K (Bottom-Up) to $808K (ARIMA)
   - Item-Level Accuracy: RMSE ~$50 per SKU per store per day
   - Department-Level Accuracy: MAPE ~8%
   - Store-Level Accuracy: MAPE ~12%
